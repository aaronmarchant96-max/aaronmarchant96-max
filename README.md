## 💡 My Core Method: CARDO REI

Whether I'm testing an AI chatbot or organizing historical family records, I use a simple 8-step framework called **CARDO REI** to keep everything accurate:

* **C**ollect — Gather all facts and data without jumping to early conclusions.
* **A**nalyze — Look for patterns and figure out what the data actually proves.
* **R**ecord — Keep clear notes and evidence trails for every single finding.
* **D**istinguish — Separate solid facts from assumptions or guesses.
* **O**rganize — Structure information so anyone can easily read and review it.
* **R**eview — Double-check for mistakes or missing details.
* **E**valuate — Assign confidence ratings and weigh the pros and cons.
* **I**terate — Keep testing and refining until it works reliably.
